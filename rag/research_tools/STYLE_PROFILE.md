# Research slides: Shuo-Hung Research Presentation Style v1

Source: user's five original September–October 2026 slide decks and saved
`Shuo_Hung_PPT_Style_Profile.json`. The original editable template is
`Shuo_Hung_Research_Presentation_Template.pptx`. These personal artifacts
are **not** committed to this public repository.

- Format: 16:9, 13.333 x 7.5 inches.
- Preferred: preserve existing PPTX master/theme, not merely approximate it.
- Plain white/light background, black Times New Roman; technical figure labels may use Arial.
- No decorative cards, gradients or icons. Red only for selective scientific emphasis.
- Cover: left aligned large research title; exact presenter line:
  `Presenter: Shuo-Hung, Wang`.
- Content: title near upper edge, original recorded rectangle
  `(x=1.53, y=0.15, w=10.89, h=0.74)` inches.
- Page number lower-right, recorded rectangle
  `(x=9.42, y=6.95, w=3.00, h=0.40)` inches.
- Title naming: concise topic + data/assay/method/result;
  e.g. `Fe3O4 Raman data`, `2-NI-4-BPA LC-MS data`.
- Typical progression: cover, completed/in-progress summary, topic divider,
  experiments/results, conclusions & future work, thank you, supplement.
- English technical headings; Chinese/English mixed body when appropriate.
- Preserve figure provenance and unconfirmed interpretations as hypotheses.
- When a user-provided full template is loaded, sample slides are removed but
  the theme/master/layouts remain. This is not a pixel-perfect guarantee: slide
  layouts, placeholders, margins and fonts should be visually QA-checked.
- The first MVP uses editable text boxes; spectrum plots are PNG images, not
  embedded editable native PowerPoint chart series. Native chart export is later work.
